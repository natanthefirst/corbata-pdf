INSTALLING CORBATA PDF ON A MAC
===============================

Corbata PDF needs macOS 13 (Ventura) or newer and runs on Apple Silicon Macs (M1 and later).


1. Copy the app into Applications
---------------------------------
In this window, drag "Corbata PDF" onto the "Applications" folder next to it.

Then eject this disk (the "Corbata PDF" icon on your desktop or in the Finder sidebar)
and open your Applications folder. Don't run the app from this disk.


2. Open it the first time
-------------------------
Corbata PDF isn't notarized by Apple yet, so the first time you open it macOS stops it
with a message like "Apple could not verify 'Corbata PDF' is free of malware".
You only have to get past this once.

  a. Double-click Corbata PDF in Applications. When the warning appears, click "Done"
     (or "OK"), not "Move to Trash".
  b. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
  c. Scroll down to the Security section. Next to "Corbata PDF was blocked...",
     click "Open Anyway", and confirm with your password or Touch ID.
  d. Click "Open" in the next window. From now on it opens normally.

On macOS 13 or 14 you can do this instead: right-click (or Control-click)
Corbata PDF in Applications, choose "Open", then click "Open".

If macOS says the app "is damaged and can't be opened":
open Terminal (Applications > Utilities > Terminal), paste this line and press Return:

    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Corbata PDF.app"

Then open the app again.


3. Make it your default PDF app (optional)
------------------------------------------
The first time Corbata PDF starts, it asks whether to become your default app for PDF files.
You can also choose File > Make Default PDF App at any time. macOS asks you to confirm.

Or do it in Finder: select any PDF, choose File > Get Info, pick Corbata PDF under
"Open with", and click "Change All...".


Uninstalling
------------
Drag Corbata PDF from Applications to the Trash.
